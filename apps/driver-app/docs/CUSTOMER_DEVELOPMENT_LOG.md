# 📱 CUSTOMER APP MASTER DEVELOPMENT & AUDIT LOG

---

## 🚀 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & VERIFIED CAPABILITIES

The Customer App (`apps/customer-app`) is a complete, multi-vertical SuperApp supporting 11 mobility and logistics services with zero mock data. All flows operate against real backend microservices and Supabase PostgreSQL database.

---

## 📋 2. VERIFIED CUSTOMER CAPABILITIES & SCREENS

1. **Authentication & Profile**: Phone OTP authentication, device trust binding, multi-profile support (Personal vs Corporate).
2. **Cab Point-to-Point**: Instant dynamic fare estimate, surge calculation, vehicle tier selection (Economy, Comfort, Premium), 4-digit pickup PIN, live driver tracking.
3. **Parcel Logistics**: Sender and receiver address & contact separation, package dimensions and fragile toggle, declared value transit insurance, 2-phase distinct OTPs.
4. **Transport & Freight**: Heavy load specifications, dimensional volume (CFT), loading/unloading helper count, multi-transporter quote review and counter-offers.
5. **Airport Transfers**: Live flight lookup (AI123), terminal pickup bay selection, auto-shifting pickup buffer for delayed flights, meet & greet placard request.
6. **Hourly Car Rental**: 1h/10km, 2h/20km, 4h/40km, 8h/80km packages, dynamic multi-stop waypoints, server-side overage timer.
7. **Outstation Tours**: One-way, round-trip, multi-city itineraries, daily driver allowances, night halts, in-app Fastag toll review and approvals.
8. **Intercity Carpool**: Highway corridor discovery, seat-by-seat reservation, CO2 emissions savings display, passenger boarding OTP.
9. **Packers & Movers**: Room inventory checklist (1BHK/2BHK/Villa), floor stair surcharges, carpenter assembly and fragile packing add-ons, damage signoff.
10. **Corporate Travel**: Enterprise profile switching, department cost center selection (CC-ENG-904), policy compliance checks, manager approval routing.
11. **Hotel Hospitality**: 5-star hotel discovery, 12% GST room booking, QR check-in voucher, optional linked airport transfer.
12. **Safety & SOS**: 112 Police panic button with live GPS coordinates, 3-hour tokenized live trip share URL (zero PII), passive route anomaly resolution ("I'm Safe").
13. **Payments & Wallet**: Multi-bucket wallet (cash, promo credit, referral rewards, pending refunds), instant top-up, itemized transparent receipts, driver tipping.
